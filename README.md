## 💫 About Me

CS student at **SRM IST, Chennai** (2024–2028) who builds **cloud and backend systems on AWS** that work end to end, from the data pipeline to the map on your screen.

- 🌧️ Building **Varshik**: AI flood-risk mapping that finds the roads that will hold water *before* the rain. Personal, because I lived through the 2015 Chennai floods.
- ☁️ Former **AWS Student Builder Campus Leader**: grew a 300+ student cloud community and was recognized among the best campus leaders.
- 🌱 Open-source contributor at **AOSSIE**
- 💼 Software Engineering Intern at **Novac Technology Solutions** (Shriram Finance Group): shipped modules of a production CRM managing 20+ construction projects
- 🎤 Hosted a **Google Cloud workshop for 150 students** and Git/GitHub sessions through the CHiPSET technical club
- 💡 Inventor on a patent application (shipment clustering and route optimization)
- 🏆 10+ hackathons: won the **PipeSense Datathon**, Top 5 of 300 at **IIT Delhi Ideathon 2026**, Design Finalist at **IN-SPACe CANSAT**
- 📜 **Microsoft Certified: SQL Server Professional**

When I'm not building, I'm probably crocheting for my little store, [**Whiskerknots**](https://www.instagram.com/whiskerknotscrochet/) 🧶.
